# Expected payment dates

Invoices and Cash Flow → Open invoices show **Expected payment**, separately from contractual due dates. Both views expose a searchable, sortable **Client payment history** section. There is no client-count cap. CSV exports include the expected date.

The read-only Convex query derives history from saved incoming bank transactions for the last 365 operating-calendar days. Bank ingestion, corrections and confirmed invoice matches change the next result; page loads never fetch upstream bank reports. It uses the current bank connection and identity version. The bounded query withholds totals and estimates if its 3,000-row / 6 MiB read budget is exceeded. Such a state requires a paginated aggregate implementation before increasing the window; it must not be treated as zero revenue. Imported history coverage is not a claim that every bank has a full year of data.

External clients are resolved from the saved client directory and exact payer aliases. QuoteLab is MediaAlpha's trading identity ([official disclosure](https://insurance-agent.mediaalpha.com/privacy)). Additional known payer spellings cover QuinStreet, SmartFinancial, LeadPier, Lead Economy, EverQuote and All Web Leads. Own-company names are tested against payer identity, never recipient text in a bank description. Capital movements, transfers and nonbusiness categories do not qualify. Business revenue includes direct media buying, affiliate-team and partner-network revenue, including all three for BuyGoods and ClickBank. Unidentified payers, unverified bank identities and one-cent verification deposits are omitted, with a receipt exclusion count in the information control. Financial records themselves are never edited by forecasting.

A forecast needs three independent fully settled invoices in the same client/currency group, using at most the latest ten bank payment events. The interval is calendar days from **invoice date** to **final bank receipt date**. Recorded/manual paid dates are not evidence. Every allocation must have an identifiable posted/settled bank receipt for the same client and currency. Cash allocation totals cannot exceed that receipt. Recorded settlement adjustments settle invoices but never increase reported cash. Pre-invoice receipts, missing links and ambiguous duplicate payment evidence cannot train the estimate. Migration surrogate IDs are not treated as verified provider identities; a linked surrogate colliding with a current receipt also disqualifies that receipt from invoice-timing observations.

The point estimate is the rounded median delay. The information control shows sample count and observed minimum/maximum, and calls out fewer than five observations. A draft uses its provisional issue date; edits recalculate it. A past estimate stays in the past and is visibly marked for follow-up. Review states do not invent future dates. Paid invoices retain their recorded paid-date display.

Receipt interval is a separate statistic: the median gap between distinct receipt dates, requiring three dates. Monthly deposits do not prove net-30 or net-45 invoice terms. Clients without enough matched history show **No estimate**, while contractual due dates remain visible. Match the historical paid invoices to their receipts through the existing invoice payment controls to improve coverage.

## Historical reconciliation

The service-authenticated `POST /api/internal/merit/history` retrieves an explicit period of at most 90 days. Validated complete responses are retained in `meritHistoryReports` with source coverage and fetch time. Repeated reads reuse saved history; corrections require an intentional `refresh: true` request. Each period has a lease and five-minute cooldown. Failed or malformed responses never replace saved data. This is a period cache, not a claim that all Merit history has been imported.

`historicalPayments.reconcile` attaches bank evidence to existing fully settled invoices under an optimistic state revision check. It preserves invoice balances, allocation amounts, adjustments and creation dates. It checks active bank identity, client, currency, exact cash amount, duplicate receipt claims and invoice locks. Deliberate old Wise import repairs additionally require the identical underlying transfer ID, account, entity, date and amount. Uncertain fees, split payments and invoices created after receipts are not new timing evidence. Reconciliation does not create payments or call upstream banks.

`historicalPayments.importSettled` imports selected originals from those saved Merit periods only when Merit confirms full settlement and an unused bank receipt matches client, currency and amount exactly. Imported invoices retain original issue/due dates and are fully paid, so open receivables and bank cash do not increase. `historicalImportedAt` keeps these deliberately imported records outside ordinary sync coverage: they neither expand routine source pulls nor disappear when a recent-period sync omits them. Historical corrections require refreshing the explicit source period and reviewing its affected matches; they never silently rewrite closed invoices. Source invoice IDs, period coverage and receipt IDs remain attached to the saved evidence.
