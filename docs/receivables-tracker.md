# Receivables tracker

Cash Flow → Open invoices → Receivables tracker keeps receipt expectations alongside the original invoice list. Draft deletion and the separate bank-pattern/invoice-history forecasts remain available. The tracker has This week, Overdue, Merit invoices, ACP + HCP, To invoice, Tariq follow-ups, Needs review and Received views. Search, sorting and view selections live in the URL; CSV exports preserve native currencies and date provenance.

## Accounting and dates

An invoice expectation references its existing invoice and payment allocations. A non-invoice expectation owns `receivableReceipts`; only its remaining amount is mirrored into `dashboardState.manualReceivables` under a reserved `tracker:` ID. Position, existing receivables totals and partner reporting therefore consume one balance. The ordinary dashboard save transaction protects these mirrors and checks receipt availability against tracker allocations. Historical settlement imports use the same reservation guard. Concurrent Convex mutations cannot claim the same cash twice. Explicit partial allocations share the available bank amount; FX allocations require separate bank/native amounts and evidence. Linking a later invoice transfers the active receipts atomically, removes the standalone mirror and preserves actual credited dates. Original cash entries are never edited by the tracker.

Dates use Asia/Beirut calendar days. Tuesday is due; Wednesday is grace; Thursday is overdue. An existing date survives later weekly snapshots. Manual overrides retain an audit trail. Explicit source dates precede an approved calendar slot, then a supported forecast. Multi-slot schedules, ranges and historical hints require a concrete selection; they do not create obligations or contractual terms. Invoice enrollment uses existing forecasts until a report or person confirms a calendar cycle. Source-paid/payer-sent status never independently settles a balance.

Automatic matching requires a current, canonical, completed external business receipt with a reliable payer/reference, exact currency and remaining amount, confirmed receiving entity/account and a date from 14 days before to 60 days after the expectation. Matches must be unique in both directions and cannot compete with an invoice. Amount-only, partial, fee, FX, bundled, inactive-connection, pending and ambiguous cases remain for administrator review. Explicit bank reversals/corrections restore outstanding balances with retained evidence. Missing rows or failed coverage do not erase receipts. Rejected suggestions stay rejected.

## Weekly intake

Administrators can paste text/CSV or upload up to 12 PNG/JPEG/WebP screenshots (20 MB total). Text is bounded to 500 KB / 500 rows. Private files remain in Convex storage and are served through authenticated endpoints. Image recognition uses the existing server-side extraction model with zero-data-retention routing. Only green **amount cells** imply source-paid; extraction never issues invoices or allocates cash. Review preserves raw rows, source confidence, summary gaps, duplicate invoice-number lines and original-currency conflicts. Saved Merit identities and amounts are authoritative. The historical USD display column does not establish native currency.

Review and save accepts validated rows and retains unresolved rows in the actor's draft. Sources have stable payer/period/channel/type identities across weekly versions; omissions do not delete balances. ACP and HCP combine separately for Leads and Clicks, with distinct streams/dates retained. Cumulative revisions preserve previous receipts and original dates. A source total below already-received cash requires review. A previously saved row is immutable within that report; pending rows can still be corrected and saved. Older reports cannot overwrite newer source periods. Existing invoice/revenue-derived draft overlaps require reconciliation, never a second accounting invoice.

Telegram `/weekly_update` scopes the next report to the authorized administrator for 30 minutes. An album uses its media-group ID and a five-minute continuation window; unrelated albums/documents keep the existing document/Amex route. Retry identity is based on actor, report week and content hashes. Shared saved items are visible to authorized Finance readers; unsaved drafts remain actor-private. Telegram links open the authenticated dashboard for review and mutation.

## Tasks and delivery

One monthly invoice-to-send task per account is created for SF Ishan, Madrivo Makar, Llama/Cyclopps and Healthy Labs, including zero/unknown amounts. Tasks do not add receivables. Linking/sending the existing invoice completes the task. SF OLM and LeadPier OLM use the Tariq workflow: a receipt comes first, then To invoice; Checked today never settles the item.

Settings selects up to three existing authorized private Finance chats, independently of partner distributions. The requested rollout recipients are Ali, Ali M and Alaa. No configured recipients means delivery is off. Dashboard-only administrators with a configured private chat can receive notifications without expanding their existing Telegram command permissions. The minute scheduler revalidates recipients before each send. Per-recipient durable outbox identities prevent replay; explicit rejection/rate-limit failures retry independently with bounded delays. Interrupted/unknown delivery becomes visible as Uncertain and requires confirmation that the message was not delivered before retrying. Historical/pre-enable bank settlements are suppressed.

Monthly, Monday/Wednesday/Friday Tariq and newly-overdue reminders use the configured Beirut time (09:00 default), with a 15-minute retry window and durable event keys. Overdue events are grouped per recipient. The existing contractual `/overdue` stays unchanged; `/expected_overdue` is separate. `/menu` also offers `/this_week`, `/pending_invoices`, `/to_invoice`, `/tariq`, `/acp_hcp`, `/weekly_update` and `/received`.

## Storage and operation

New tables are additive: items, source rows, report drafts, receipts, audit, settings, deliveries and intake sessions. They preserve existing invoices and the bank ledger. Saved-bank matching runs with the existing five-minute server workflow; invoice enrollment and rules use stored data. No dashboard refresh calls banking or Merit upstream. Bank matching queries the existing direction/currency/amount index for open balances, with at most 1,000 candidates per amount bucket and 3,000 total; only the last 400 days are eligible. An exceeded bound returns an incomplete result and records no new matches. Invoice-only trackers skip bank history reads. Consumed receipt identities are decoded once, including historical Wise equivalents. Tracker job failures do not block the separate invoice matcher. Active item and receipt bounds are explicit (2,000 and 5,000); limits fail visibly instead of presenting partial totals. Report history displays the latest 30 actor drafts; item audit displays its latest 100 events, with durable older evidence retained. Private uploads, source rows and audit remain retained until a deliberate authorized data-deletion/archival operation; there is no automatic financial-data purge.
